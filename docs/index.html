Weather by the hour

Each play gets the temperature, rain, snow, humidity and wind recorded at its stadium in that hour (UTC), so a game that turns cold in the fourth quarter is counted that way.

Indoor games

Dome and closed-roof games count as 70°F with no wind, rain or snow, and skip the humidity filter. Open roofs use the outdoor weather.

Expected points added

EPA measures how much a play changed a team's expected score. Above zero helped the offense, below zero hurt it.
